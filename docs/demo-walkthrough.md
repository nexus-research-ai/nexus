## Scope of this demonstration

- Login opens a local demo workspace; it does not validate a production account or password. Do not enter a real password.
- Posts, availability, follows, notification read state, and recent chats are stored in this browser. Rehearsal actions can remain visible on the next take.
- Researcher personas, project descriptions, messages, match scores, activity counts, and example conversations are demonstration content. A private interest click records local intent; it does not contact a real researcher or prove mutual acceptance. The introduction dialog explicitly models Jordan’s prior acceptance as a fixture.
- Literature search uses the imported public paper corpus, with source links and imported titles, authors, and abstracts. These records are distinct from the fictional workspace personas and posts. Importing metadata does not independently verify publication status, author identity, peer review, or research claims; inspect the original source before citing it.
- The graph visualizes the available local data. Funding cards are an example catalog; check the agency’s current solicitation before relying on any opportunity, budget, eligibility, or deadline.
- Recent conversations are saved in this browser. Seeded examples are marked as demo conversations. New Nexus AI prompts use the configured backend and model service; distinguish live replies from previously saved conversations in the narration.
- Vault, proposal audit, and verification screens contain additional illustrative states. This recording does not establish real blockchain registration, cryptographic provenance, source validation, or completed experiments.
