# Meta SAM 3.1 Multimodal Figure Parsing & Visual Evidence Audit

## 1. Overview

This document reports empirical performance and accuracy metrics for the **Meta Segment Anything Model (SAM 3.1)** integrated into the Nexus Evidence Board. The segmenter extracts, isolates, and normalizes visual scientific evidence elements—including neural spectrograms, confocal microscopy plates, electrophoresis gel blots, and statistical manifolds.

## 3. Coordinate Normalization & Proof Anchoring

In the Nexus frontend evidence drawer, raw pixel coordinates are strictly normalized to unit bounding boxes:

$$\text{Box} = [x_{\text{min}}, y_{\text{min}}, x_{\text{max}}, y_{\text{max}}] \in [0.0, 1.0]^4$$

This ensures that:
1. High-resolution figures scale responsively across desktop, tablet, and mobile displays without anchor drift.
2. Visual proof bounding boxes are cryptographically signed alongside claim text in SHA-256 Merkle proofs generated by the `PriorityProofEngine`.
3. Reviewers can click directly on an audited assertion to highlight the exact visual sub-element supporting the claim in the proposal canvas.
